# Human review rubric — prepared protocol component

This rubric prepares the later independent team review. No study responses have been assigned or rated by this component.

| Dimension | 0 — Does not meet expectations | 1 — Partly meets expectations | 2 — Meets expectations |
|---|---|---|---|
| Response suitability | Misses the central issue or gives an inappropriate response | Addresses the issue but leaves an important part unresolved | Gives the appropriate plan, clarification, conflict explanation, or scope response |
| Profile fit | Ignores or contradicts relevant profile information | Uses some relevant details but misses important constraints or goals | Reflects the information needed for this request |
| Practicality and completeness | Is unusable or substantially incomplete for its purpose | Provides some useful guidance but leaves important gaps | Gives sufficiently complete and practical guidance for the request |
| Evidence accuracy | Contains materially unsupported or misleading claims | Has partial support or omits important qualifications | Agrees with the reviewed evidence and communicates relevant uncertainty |
| Clarity | Is confusing or internally inconsistent | Is understandable but noticeably ambiguous | Is clear, consistent, and easy to follow |

Keep the five dimensions separate. Do not sum them into a new quality score or introduce a passing threshold after seeing final results.

## Rating states and context

Rated entries use 0, 1, or 2. Missing entries remain unscored. Only evidence accuracy may be not applicable, with a reason, when there are no substantive factual or instructional claims to assess.

When an executed job returns no response or no readably assessable content, such as empty text or uninterpretable gibberish, the four non-evidence dimensions receive zero under the rubric. Readable wrong or off-topic content is judged by each dimension. Unstarted or unresolved jobs remain pending. This component does not populate actual ratings.

Review malformed but readable text as received without repair. Wrong or off-topic content can still be clear. A clear inappropriate refusal need not receive zero for clarity.

Judge completeness and profile fit relative to the actual request; a good clarification need not invent a weekly plan, and irrelevant profile details need not be repeated.

Substantive claims in plans, refusals and scope responses require evidence review even without citations. Unverifiable is not automatically false. Distinguish contradiction, insufficient support and omitted qualifications. An omitted requested food fact remains a completeness issue.

Explain important problems and identify supporting sources when factual claims are disputed.

## Review procedure

The planned sample is 40 profiles: 25 feasible and five in each challenge category. Each age contributes five feasible and one of each challenge category. All four version outcomes give 160 responses, each reviewed independently by two actual teammates. Actual selection and assignments remain pending.

Select within age-scenario strata using stable ordering and recorded seed before generated outputs are examined. Retain adopted family information. Include all four version outcomes for each selected profile.

Use two actual teammates working independently. Practice with development examples; retain and lock initial ratings before discussion. Store adjudication separately. Randomize presentation reproducibly and preserve returned text. This is team rating, not external coaching or clinical validation; complete blinding cannot be guaranteed.

The human sample intentionally contains more challenge cases than the full benchmark. Report its composition instead of treating pooled ratings as representative of all 800 planned jobs.

Reviewers receive the public profile/request, returned response and citations, rubric, and neutral access to adopted references. Keep version labels, traces, internal evaluator findings, automated scores, timing, private scenario labels, and feasibility examples outside the packets.

## Agreement reporting

Compute exact agreement and linearly weighted Cohen's kappa separately for each dimension, using only pairs with two numeric ratings. Fix ordinal order at 0, 1, 2. Report undefined values as unavailable with their reason. Keep N/A and missing-state counts and both reviewers' distributions; report their denominators and overlaps.

## Illustrations for reading the rubric

These 15 authored illustrations concern one dimension at a time. They are not study profiles, actual reviewer decisions, or completed reviewer calibration. The room-access example uses a fictional reference. Development practice with adopted references remains pending.

### Response suitability

- **0:** A request asks for two sessions; the reply gives only an unrelated shopping list.
- **1:** The reply addresses one requested session but leaves the second unresolved.
- **2:** The reply addresses both requested sessions under the supplied facts.

### Profile fit

- **0:** The fixture states one participant and one ball; the reply requires three partners and four balls.
- **1:** The reply uses one ball but still assumes an unavailable partner.
- **2:** The reply respects the explicitly stated participant count and ball supply.

### Practicality and completeness

- **0:** The fixture needs two essential facts clarified; the reply asks only an irrelevant question.
- **1:** The clarification asks for one essential fact and leaves the other unresolved.
- **2:** The clarification asks for both essential facts without inventing a weekly plan.

### Evidence accuracy

- **0:** A fictional reference permits room use Tuesday and Thursday by reservation; the reply claims unrestricted daily access.
- **1:** The reply gives the two correct days but omits the reservation qualification.
- **2:** The reply states the two days and reservation condition, and identifies the fictional reference.

### Clarity

- **0:** The reply gives mutually contradictory start and end instructions.
- **1:** The reply is understandable but leaves the intended time ambiguous.
- **2:** The reply states the intended time clearly and consistently.
