# Youth Basketball Recovery and Participation Rules v0.1

This dataset is a curated reference of published youth basketball
participation, rest, and sleep guidelines. It is not measured athlete
recovery data, an individualized training prescription, or medical advice.

## Primary References

- NBA/USA Basketball Youth Guidelines:
  https://youthguidelines.nba.com/

- USA Basketball:
  https://www.usab.com/play/youth-basketball-guidelines

- CDC Sleep Recommendations:
  https://www.cdc.gov/sleep/about/

- American Academy of Pediatrics:
  https://publications.aap.org/pediatrics/article/153/2/e2023065129/196435

## Scope and Limitations

- Preserve distinctions between maximum, minimum, and recommended-range rules.
- Organized basketball includes games, practices, and structured training.
- NBA recommendations use both age-based and grade-based categories.
- Sleep recommendations are general guidance, not medical diagnoses.
- Do not interpret a reported absence of injury as medical clearance.
- Verify applicability before activating these rules in the AI planner.
- Referenced recommendations should be reviewed if official guidance changes.
