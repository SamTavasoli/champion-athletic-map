# Live request-contract development probe

Step 31-7 prepared stricter final-response and food-note requirements and tested
them offline against the saved failures. This experiment exercises those
requirements on the local backend. It is a fresh development smoke test, not
a before/after improvement estimate or a final study run.

The fixed selected public case is athlete:46a43835d1a3112756ee7181, the previously
versioned fictional 16-year-old beginner. It asks for two additional finishing
footwork practices, has two 15-minute available windows, and allows two sessions
and thirty additional minutes for the week. Two existing basketball practices
must be preserved. Four participants, half-court access, one ball, four cones and
one hoop are stated. Its optional factual question asks for the recorded protein
in 250 g of raw blueberries. This case was not used in the preceding recorded
operational probes. The request, maximum and slot count are all two; this probe
does not by itself retest count-versus-upper-limit confusion.

The experiment uses the prepared planner order, same base model and generation
options, original output/context reserves, complete-request budget check and
two-revision V4 policy. At most fifteen generation submissions are permitted.
Existing retries, repairs and best-of selection are not added. Each workflow's
designated terminal result remains its outcome. An execution failure does not
permit substitution of an earlier draft.

The prospective request-contract runtime is selected through a private loaded
runner module. The tested runner is reused with distinct manifest, call and
result identities. The artifact manifest uses a new contract_<digest> ID under
the existing shared_guidance artifact directory because the tested manifest
validator uses that directory. Original summaries, job outcomes, schemas,
instruction files and configurations are unchanged.

All four planners use the same public-derived final contract, including V4
revisions. Food-only specialist contracts apply to the two specialist drafting
workflows. Planner 1 receives no lookup values, reference packet or assessment
feedback during generation. Reference-enabled workflows prepare matched complete
packets. Backend acceptance and observed native-schema conformance are recorded
separately from strict local calendar validation and independent semantic checks.
An enum of allowed dates does not enforce chronological order or uniqueness.
A required food statement can honestly report unknown information and still
leave factual answer completeness unresolved.

The existing independent assessor checks all designated saved outcomes after
generation. A valid format does not establish requested-session fulfilment,
preserved practices, resource fit or a correct food value. Finishing-footwork
descriptions can remain outside the three reviewed resource grammars; retain
that limitation without calling it a verified physical shortage or triggering
another model retry.

Exact completed jobs are reusable on rerun. Incomplete or uncertain requests
remain preserved and stop automatic submission. This small probe records no
accepted study profiles, human ratings, final scores, app activation or protocol
freeze. Use the resulting evidence to finish the broader pilot settings and
scoring preparation rather than repeatedly regenerating this exposed case.
