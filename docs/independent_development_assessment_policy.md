# Independent development checks — candidate policy

We use this checkpoint to assess the saved development replies outside the generation workflow. It records what the replies contain, what the software can establish, and what still needs review. It makes no new model request and does not change the original records, internal acceptance decisions, or frozen Step 17 scoring contract.

We retain the four original probe outcomes. The amended Version 4 reply is a separate development continuation with its own request and response hashes. It does not replace the original execution failure in a matched comparison. These five diagnostic records are not five planner versions or a completed experiment. We do not calculate final success rates from this one exposed case.

Our interpretation follows the prepared planner assessment, evidence accounting, and human review contracts in `artifacts/evaluation/scoring_config.json`, `docs/evaluation_protocol.md`, and `docs/human_review_rubric.md`. Their accepted-case masks, final population, calibration, human assignments, and protocol readiness remain pending. The obligations below are a versioned development mask; they do not populate or silently approve the final `case_check_masks`.

## What each finding means

We use `met`, `violated`, `unknown`, and justified `not_applicable` for assessed conditions. An established violation prevents that parent condition from passing. Necessary unknowns also prevent a pass. Missing assessment rows, an unavailable parser, or a checker exception are assessment failures, recorded separately from a model error. An empty checklist cannot establish success.

We preserve the exact returned text, including malformed but readable replies, and identify the assessed response by its hash. The common checks run outside all four planners. Version 4's internal evaluator and acceptance flag do not assign the independent result. This is software assessment and source review under the recorded development scope; it is not an independent human rating or clinical approval.

For a supported non-plan response, schedule-specific obligations need an explicit public basis for non-applicability. An unjustified refusal or clarification cannot waive the requested plan. Missing optional history is not a blanket failure. Activities and claims actually present still receive the applicable checks.

For this specific exposed case, the public input states age 13, grade 8, two requested extra sessions, a maximum of three sessions and 60 minutes for the week, a 20-minute session maximum, confirmed empty existing commitments, and a protein question about 100 g of raw strawberries. The development-required mask therefore includes a suitable complete plan, the calendar/accounting/commitment checks, exactly two added root sessions, the supplied time and resource constraints, the applicable age-band basketball cap/rest checks, `food_answer_completeness`, and `food_numeric_correctness`. Grade-9–12 bounds receive justified non-applicability for the stated grade. Other advisory guidance and optional attribution measures remain separate. The three resource-related legacy rows are linked findings, not three independent feasibility failures. The new declaration and requirement-evidence findings remain distinct, and both are needed for complete resource verification.

## Positive resource evidence has a limited scope

The initial independent resource contracts cover three concrete source-base variants: stationary low/middle dribbling, partnered close-range one-hand form shooting, and a partnered rolled-ball defensive slide. They assess the reviewed actions, equipment, active roles, rotation and stated space constraints. Matching may use concrete returned content without a catalog ID; an ID by itself is insufficient. The same matching and capacity rules apply to every planner.

Extra unsupported movement, unresolved role arrangements, an unrecognized variant, or unreviewed narrative space restrictions stay unknown. These contracts do not establish every activity's feasibility, appropriate training dose, supervision, coaching suitability, or medical safety. The saved generic session descriptions do not become verified merely because a positive-proof path now exists for other concrete descriptions.

## Food facts and source access remain distinct

All four replies can be checked against the same independent frozen food reference after generation. That external assessment does not give reference material to Version 1 during generation. Answer completeness, numeric accuracy, citation identity, attribution, and evidential support remain separate findings. Missing source IDs alone do not establish a wrong number. Unsupported prose alone does not establish a false fact. An omitted requested answer remains missing even when a source packet was available elsewhere in the workflow.

If no food source is cited, the narrow `food_source_grounding` binding check can be not applicable because there is no supplied citation to resolve. Citation presence is still recorded as absent. A source-dependent uncited claim remains in the separate attribution-review inventory; this non-applicability does not imply usable attribution, complete support, or perfect evidence coverage.

We inventory claims actually returned and record the assessment scope. Empty denominators stay unavailable, and incomplete inventories do not yield completed evidence rates. We do not invent claims to score an omitted answer.

## How we use this checkpoint

The recorded three-versus-two session contradiction, omitted food answer, self-parent links, and unsupported measurable-goal demand remain development findings. The evaluator prompt already prohibits inventing a measurable-goal rule; its failure to follow that instruction is retained model behavior. New external checks add a separate diagnostic record and do not turn any earlier failure into a pass or rewrite the historical checker output.

Before using the full 20-profile pilot to choose or freeze study settings, we still need reviewed case-specific expectations and families, representative feasibility evidence, common assessment coverage, adopted-threshold boundary fixtures, and a tested scoring ledger. These narrow contracts do not complete those requirements. Human rubric practice and two actual independent teammate reviews remain separate future work; no human score, final sample, or approval is implied here.

The accompanying shared-task addendum is a prospective candidate. We will activate it identically for all four future workflows, including nested Version 4 drafting and review, only after the common checks and resulting configuration are reviewed and versioned. Earlier development exposure and outcomes stay recorded. We will not use another response from this case to replace a weak result.
